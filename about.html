About Dr. Sheri Durricks

Compassionate, trauma-informed, faith-centered leadership in healing care.

Dr. Sheri Durricks is a Licensed Professional Counselor, author, advocate, and founder of Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC — a faith-integrated, trauma-informed private practice dedicated to helping individuals heal beyond survival mode and rediscover emotional wellness, personal restoration, and renewed purpose.

For more than fifteen years, Dr. Durricks has served individuals navigating trauma, anxiety, grief, emotional exhaustion, relationship challenges, leadership pressure, and life transitions. Her passion centers around supporting high-functioning men and women who often appear strong outwardly while silently carrying overwhelming emotional burdens internally.

Dr. Durricks believes healing is both sacred and transformative. Her therapeutic approach combines trauma-informed counseling, emotional wellness, personal empowerment, and faith-integrated support to help individuals restore balance, strengthen identity, rebuild resilience, and reconnect with peace and clarity.

As founder, she has developed therapeutic workbooks, stabilization tools, and supportive healing resources for emotional regulation, leadership sustainability, identity restoration, and long-term growth. Her work also extends through Above & Beyond Intercessory Prayer Ministry.

She is the author of The Many Faces of Domestic Violence: A Woman’s Guide from Trauma to Triumph and founder of Beauty4URAshes, an empowerment initiative supporting survivors in reclaiming voice, confidence, identity, and purpose.

Her advocacy includes evangelism outreach, homeless initiatives, shelter support services, mentoring, and community teaching throughout Camden, New Jersey. Dr. Durricks earned her master’s degree from Lincoln University and is a graduate of the Scotland School for Veterans’ Children.

Today, she continues creating emotionally safe, faith-centered spaces where healing becomes possible, identity is restored, and individuals move forward with strength, clarity, wholeness, and hope.

Healing with dignity, clarity, and sacred care.
