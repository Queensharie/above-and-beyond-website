Dr. Sheri Durricks

Trauma Specialist • Counselor • Author • Advocate • Visionary Leader

Dr. Sheri Durricks is a faith-integrated trauma specialist, counselor, author, advocate, and visionary leader dedicated to helping individuals heal beyond survival and rediscover wholeness, identity, and emotional freedom. As the founder of Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC, Dr. Durricks has devoted more than fifteen years to walking alongside high-functioning men and women who silently carry the weight of trauma, leadership pressure, grief, emotional exhaustion, and life transitions while appearing strong to the world around them.

Known for her compassionate yet empowering approach, Dr. Durricks creates spaces where clinical excellence, emotional safety, spiritual sensitivity, and authentic healing coexist. Her work is grounded in the belief that individuals should never have to choose between their faith, their humanity, and their healing journey. Through trauma-informed counseling, emotional stabilization tools, leadership support, and restorative care, she helps clients cultivate resilience, clarity, emotional regulation, and renewed purpose.

Dr. Durricks is the creator of therapeutic workbooks and transformational healing resources designed to support emotional wellness, identity restoration, and sustainable personal growth. She is also the founder of Above & Beyond Intercessory Prayer Ministry, reflecting her commitment to holistic healing that honors both emotional and spiritual restoration.

In addition to her clinical work, Dr. Durricks is the author of The Many Faces of Domestic Violence: A Woman’s Guide from Trauma to Triumph and the founder of Beauty4URAshes, an empowerment movement and talk show that amplifies the voices of survivors while promoting healing, resilience, dignity, and restoration. Her advocacy extends deeply into the community through outreach initiatives, evangelism, mentorship, feeding the homeless, and providing emotional support and resources to underserved populations throughout Camden, New Jersey.

Dr. Durricks earned her master’s degree from Lincoln University and is a proud graduate of the Scotland School for Veterans’ Children, experiences that helped shape her lifelong commitment to discipline, leadership, service, advocacy, and community empowerment.

With a passion for advancing trauma-informed care and faith integration, Dr. Durricks continues to expand her work through counseling, speaking engagements, educational resources, leadership development, and community outreach. Her mission is to build transformative spaces where healing becomes attainable, identity is restored, and individuals are empowered to rise above adversity with strength, dignity, clarity, and purpose.
