Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC

Dr. Sheri Durricks

Dr. Sheri Durricks is a Licensed Professional Counselor, author, advocate, and founder of Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC — a faith-integrated, trauma-informed private practice dedicated to helping individuals heal beyond survival mode and rediscover emotional wellness, personal restoration, and renewed purpose.

For more than fifteen years, Dr. Durricks has served individuals navigating trauma, anxiety, grief, emotional exhaustion, relationship challenges, leadership pressure, and life transitions. Her passion centers around supporting high-functioning men and women who often appear strong outwardly while silently carrying overwhelming emotional burdens internally. Through compassionate counseling and emotionally restorative care, she provides a safe, confidential space where individuals no longer have to suffer in silence or carry life’s weight alone.

Dr. Durricks believes healing is both sacred and transformative. Her therapeutic approach combines trauma-informed counseling, emotional wellness, personal empowerment, and faith-integrated support to help individuals restore balance, strengthen identity, rebuild resilience, and reconnect with peace and clarity.

As the founder of Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC, Dr. Durricks has also developed therapeutic workbooks, stabilization tools, and supportive healing resources designed to encourage emotional regulation, leadership sustainability, identity restoration, and long-term personal growth. Her work extends beyond the counseling office through Above & Beyond Intercessory Prayer Ministry, an outreach committed to providing spiritual encouragement, prayer support, and holistic care for leaders and individuals from all walks of life.

Dr. Durricks is also the author of The Many Faces of Domestic Violence: A Woman’s Guide from Trauma to Triumph, a trauma-informed resource created to educate, empower, and support survivors on their healing journey. In addition, she founded Beauty4URAshes, an empowerment initiative and talk show dedicated to helping survivors of abuse and trauma reclaim their voice, confidence, identity, and sense of purpose.

Her dedication to advocacy and restoration extends deeply into the community through evangelism outreach, homeless initiatives, shelter support services, mentoring, and community teaching throughout Camden, New Jersey. She remains committed to serving underserved populations with compassion, dignity, encouragement, and practical support.

Dr. Durricks earned her master’s degree from Lincoln University and is a proud graduate of the Scotland School for Veterans’ Children, where early experiences in leadership, discipline, service, and resilience helped shape her lifelong commitment to helping others heal and thrive.

Today, through Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC, Dr. Sheri Durricks continues to create emotionally safe, faith-centered spaces where healing becomes possible, identity is restored, and individuals are empowered to move forward with strength, clarity, wholeness, and hope.
