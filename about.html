Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC

Dr. Sheri Durricks

Above & Beyond Counseling & Consulting LLC was created for the individuals who carry everyone else — yet silently struggle to carry themselves.

Behind leadership, caregiving, ministry, strength, and responsibility, many people quietly battle emotional exhaustion, anxiety, trauma, grief, relationship strain, and the pressure of always being “the strong one.”

Here, you do not have to perform, pretend, or hold everything together.

Above & Beyond provides a safe, confidential, faith-integrated space where healing begins with honesty, restoration, and the freedom to finally exhale.

Through trauma-informed counseling and compassionate support, I help men and women:

  • rebuild emotional balance
  • strengthen their faith
  • heal from past wounds
  • rediscover peace
  • reconnect with themselves

This is more than counseling.
This is a journey toward wholeness.

You are not weak for needing support.
You are human.

And healing begins the moment you no longer have to carry it all alone.
